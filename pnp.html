⚙️ 0. GAME SETUP STANDARD TABLETOP DISPOSITION
1. Reference Sheets: Give each player 1 double-sided Reference Sheet (Front: Field Directive Index; Reverse: Conceptual Reference Matrix).
2. Buzzer & Tokens: Place the Giant Foam Buzzer in the center of the table, with the reserve bank of 44 Point Tokens beside it.
3. Deck: Shuffle the 50 Anomaly Cards and place them face-down as the draw deck.

1. ROUND SETUP (INITIALIZATION)

1. Reveal 5 to 7 cards face-up in the center of the table.

2. Memorize (15s): All players observe and memorize the cards. Then turn them face-down and shuffle them together (induced amnesia).

3. The Anomaly: The Active Researcher secretly looks at one of these cards without showing anyone. This card is the Anomaly.

2. GIVING CLUES (THE DEGRADATION LOOP)

The Researcher puts 1 Point Token on their Matrix to start. Then, recite clues rhythmically in a continuous loop:

1. Action: "It does not [Verb]" (e.g., does not fly)
2. State: "It is not [Adjective]" (e.g., is not soft)
3. Anatomy: "It has no [Property]" (e.g., has no wheels)
4. Taxonomy: "It does not belong to [Category]" (e.g., not food)

• Escalating Stakes: Every time the Researcher completes all 4 lines, add +1 Point Token and immediately start the next loop.

🚨 BREACH: Naming any positive property causes an immediate breach: the round ends instantly with 0 points for everyone!

3. GUESSING & SCORING (INTERCEPTION)

• Hit the Buzzer Anytime: Any player can slam the buzzer at any moment—even mid-sentence! The Researcher must stop speaking immediately.

• 1 Guess per Round: Each player gets exactly one guess per round.

❌ Wrong Guess (Lockdown): Rotate your Reference Sheet 90° sideways. You cannot speak or guess again this round. The Researcher resumes the loop from where they were interrupted.

⚠️ LAST PLAYER STANDING RULE:
If all guessing players except one are locked out, the round ends immediately! The last remaining player loses the chance to guess (you cannot wait safely for everyone else to fail!).

🟢 Correct Guess (Mutual Survival): The Researcher claims all tokens on their Matrix; the guessing player gets an equal amount from the reserve. Pass the Researcher role clockwise.

🔴 Total Erasure: If everyone fails (or the round is closed early by the Last Player rule), the Anomaly escapes. 0 points awarded.

🏆 VICTORY: First player to reach 7 Points wins immediately!

🧪 AUTONOMOUS TESTING PROTOCOL (SOLO PLAYTEST DRILL) MODE: QUARANTINE SIMULATOR

Field Agents can test and calibrate the game completely autonomously after printing with this individual verification drill:

  1. Amnesia Setup: Take 6 random Subject Cards from the archive, memorize them for 20 seconds, and place them face down in a row.
  2. Inverse Negative Synthesis: Draw the target anomaly card blindly (or use the companion site). Formulate aloud 3 complete Degradation Loops (exactly 12 negative constraints: 3 Actions, 3 States, 3 Anatomies, 3 Taxonomies) that distinguish the target without naming positive traits.
  3. Timed Interception: Start a 15-second countdown timer, flip the 6 face-down cards, and identify the Anomaly instantly using only your negative parameters. If correct on the first attempt, claim the loop yield points (1 to 3 tokens). If you hesitate or misidentify, mark 1 Containment Breach. Reach 7 Yield Points before 3 breaches to achieve solo certification!
