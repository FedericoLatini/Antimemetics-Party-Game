STEP 0

GAME SETUP

INITIAL TABLETOP DISPOSITION
📇 1. PLAYER REFERENCE SHEETS

Give each player one double-sided Reference Sheet: the Field Directive Index on the front, and the Conceptual Reference Matrix (with loop stems & token track) on the back.

🚨 2. BUZZER & POINT TOKENS

Place the Giant Foam Buzzer in the center of the table, with the reserve bank of 44 Point Tokens right beside it within reach of all players.

📦 3. SHUFFLE DRAW DECK

Thoroughly shuffle all 50 Anomaly Cards and place them face-down as the draw deck.

01
PHASE 1

Round Setup (Initialization)

Reveal 5 to 7 cards face-up in the center. All players observe and memorize them for 15 seconds. Turn them face-down and shuffle them together (amnesia). The Active Researcher secretly looks at one: this is the Anomaly.

Keep the memory pool face-down in front of all players. Only the Researcher knows which card was drawn.
02
PHASE 2

Giving Clues (The Loop)

The Researcher places 1 Point Token on their sheet to start. Recite 4 strictly negative statements rhythmically: Action, State, Anatomy, Taxonomy. Every full loop completed adds +1 Point Token!

🚨 BREACH: Saying any positive property causes an immediate breach: round ends instantly with 0 points for everyone!
03
PHASE 3

Guessing & Interception

Hit the buzzer at any time (even mid-sentence) → the Researcher must stop speaking immediately. Each player gets 1 guess per round. If wrong, rotate card 90° (Lockdown). The Researcher resumes the loop!

⚠️ LAST PLAYER RULE: If all guessing players except one are locked out, the round ends immediately (0 pts)!
04
PHASE 4

Scoring & Victory

• Correct Guess: Both the Researcher and the guesser claim the accumulated tokens.
• Total Erasure: If everyone fails or round closes early, 0 points are awarded.
• First to 7 Points wins immediately!

🏆 Reaching 7 Points achieves Memetic Stabilization and wins the game!
ANOMALY SIMULATION TERMINAL

The Degradation Loop Laboratory

Select any subject from the 50 authorized archives to inspect its deconstruction across the 4 strict negative vectors.

1. ACTION
"It does not [Verb]" What the anomaly cannot or does not do (e.g., does not fly, does not float, does not ring)
2. STATE
"It is not [Adjective]" Physical quality absent (e.g., is not soft, is not transparent, is not fragile)
3. ANATOMY
"It has no [Property]" Missing physical structural parts (e.g., has no wheels, has no cables, has no display screen)
4. TAXONOMY
"It does not belong to [Category]" Excluded classification class (e.g., not food, not a vehicle, not apparel)
