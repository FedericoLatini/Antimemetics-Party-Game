## 🗂️ Project Overview

**"There Is No Antimemetics Party Game"** is a high-tension party game of negative deduction, localized amnesia, and cognitive reflexes for **3 to 7 Field Agents** (with a dedicated **Solo Containment Drill** mode).

In this game, conceptual information is dangerous and volatile. The Active Researcher cannot state what the Anomaly is: they must communicate it **strictly through what it is NOT**, reciting a rigorous 4-part **Degradation Loop**. Meanwhile, Field Agents attempt to intercept the Anomaly from their fading short-term memory before informational erasure takes place, physically striking the **Soft Override Interface** (the giant foamy buzzer).

## 🕹️ Game Rules & Structure

### 0. Setup
1. **Reference Sheets:** Give each player **1 double-sided Reference Sheet** (Front: *Field Directive Index*; Reverse: *Conceptual Reference Matrix*).
2. **Central Supply:** Place the **Giant Foam Buzzer** in the center of the table, with the pool of **44 Point Tokens** beside it.
3. **Draw Deck:** Thoroughly shuffle the **50 Anomaly Cards** and place them face-down as the draw deck.

### 1. Round Setup (Initialization)
1. **Reveal 5 to 7 cards** face-up in the center of the table.
2. **Memorize (15s):** All players observe and memorize the revealed cards for 15 seconds.
3. **Amnesia:** Flip all revealed cards face-down and shuffle them together.
4. **The Target:** The Active Researcher secretly looks at **one** of these cards without showing anyone. This card is **the Anomaly**.

### 2. Giving Clues (The Degradation Loop)
The Researcher places **1 Point Token** on their Reference Sheet to start the round, then gives clues in a rhythmic loop.

Each loop consists of **4 strictly negative statements** spoken rhythmically:
* **1. Action:** *"It does not [Verb]"* — *(e.g., "It does not fly")*
* **2. State:** *"It is not [Adjective]"* — *(e.g., "It is not soft")*
* **3. Anatomy:** *"It has no [Part/Property]"* — *(e.g., "It has no wheels")*
* **4. Taxonomy:** *"It does not belong to [Category]"* — *(e.g., "It is not food")*

* **Escalating Stakes:** Every time the Researcher completes all 4 lines, they add **+1 Point Token** to their sheet and immediately begin the next loop.
* 🚨 **Containment Breach:** If the Researcher accidentally states a **positive** fact *(e.g., "It is made of metal" or "It has a handle")*, containment fails immediately! The round ends on the spot and **nobody scores any points**.

### 3. Guessing & Scoring (Interception)
Players try to identify the secret card from the memorized pool.

* **Hit the Buzzer to Interrupt:** Any player can slam the buzzer at **any time**—even mid-sentence!
  * As soon as the buzzer sounds, the Researcher **must stop speaking immediately**.
* **One Guess per Round:** Each player gets **only 1 guess** per round.

#### What happens after a guess?
* ❌ **Incorrect Guess (Cognitive Lockdown):**
  * The player rotates their Reference Sheet **90 degrees sideways**.
  * They are in **Lockdown**: they cannot speak or guess again for the rest of the round.
  * The Researcher **resumes the loop from where they were interrupted**.
* ⚠️ **The Last Player Standing Rule:**
  * **If all guessing players except one are locked out, the round ends instantly!**
  * The last remaining player **does not get to guess**. *(You cannot wait safely for everyone else to fail!)*
* 🟢 **Correct Guess (Mutual Survival):**
  * The Researcher takes all Point Tokens accumulated on their sheet.
  * The player who guessed correctly takes an **equal number of tokens** from the central supply.
  * The round is over. Pass the Researcher role clockwise.
* 🔴 **Everyone Fails (Total Erasure):**
  * If all players fail their guess (or the round ends because only one player was left), the Anomaly escapes.
  * All tokens on the sheet return to the supply: **0 points awarded**.

### 🏆 Winning the Game
The game ends the moment any player reaches **7 Points**, achieving Memetic Stabilization and winning the game!

## 🧪 Autonomous Testing Protocol (Solo Playtest Drill)

Anyone who prints the game can test and master the mechanics completely autonomously:
1. **Amnesia Setup:** Extract 6 Subject Cards from the archive, memorize them for 20 seconds, and place them face-down in a row.
2. **Inverse Negative Synthesis:** Blindly designate one card as the target (or use the companion site). Formulate aloud 3 complete Degradation Loops (exactly 12 negative constraints: 3 Actions, 3 States, 3 Anatomies, 3 Taxonomies) that isolate the target without citing positive attributes.
3. **Timed Interception:** Start a 15-second countdown timer, flip the 6 face-down cards, and identify the Anomaly instantly using only your negative parameters. If correct on the first attempt, claim the loop yield points (1 to 3 tokens). If you hesitate or misidentify, mark 1 Containment Breach. Reach 7 Yield Points before 3 breaches to achieve solo certification!
